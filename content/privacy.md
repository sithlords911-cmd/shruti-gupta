# Privacy

- **Kicker:** Privacy
- **H1:** Short version: this site collects nothing.

## What this site doesn't do
- No contact forms — the email buttons open your own mail app.
- No cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
- No accounts, no logins, no databases of yours or mine.

## What happens when you email me
Your message lands in a normal Gmail inbox (shruuspeaks@gmail.com). I read it, reply, and keep it — like any email conversation. If you'd rather I delete the thread afterward, say so and it's gone.

## Third parties
This site loads self-hosted fonts and images — no Google Fonts requests, no CDNs, nothing phones home to a third party when you browse.
