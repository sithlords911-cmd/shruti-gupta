The story

From SOPs to SaaS.

Six years ago I started freelancing for whoever would pay me to write: visa SOPs, scholarship essays, long-form tech blogs for brands I'd never heard of. Twenty brands later, the work narrowed to what I'm best at — SEO content for SaaS, AI, and tech products.

The differentiator is simple: I read your docs. My bachelor's is software development; my final master's project was a full-stack MERN app. Feature lists, REST APIs, release notes, product UIs — I understand what I'm selling in words before I write a line.

I'm also an editor. At BidGrowth Media I went from intern to senior writer/editor in two years, training interns and running QA on other people's copy. Clients get drafts that already went through an editor — because they did.
