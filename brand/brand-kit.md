# Brand kit — Shruti Gupta

**Identity in one line:** an editor's portfolio — the oxblood period is the signature: everything she writes ends properly.

## Marks
- **Wordmark (primary):** `Shruti Gupta` in Fraunces 500, ink `#1B1814`, ending in an oxblood period. Rendered as styled text in the masthead/footer — not an image.
- **Monogram (icon):** `sg.` — `brand/mark.svg` (light) · `brand/favicon.svg` (dark tile for browser chrome). Legible ≥ 24px; minimum clear space = the period's diameter ×4.
- **Dark variant:** ink → `#FAF7F2`, period → `#C65D42` (lighter oxblood for contrast on dark).

## Palette
See `theme/tokens.json` — paper `#FAF7F2` / ink `#1B1814` / oxblood accent `#7A2E1F` / hairline `#DDD6C8`. Accent usage: links, kickers, the period, ONE primary CTA — never a wash.

## Type
- Fraunces — display/wordmark (400–600)
- Source Serif 4 — prose
- Inter — UI/meta (kicker = 0.75rem uppercase, 0.14em tracking)
- Self-hosted woff2, `font-display: swap`

## Voice (locked — applies to every string)
Confident, plain-spoken, first-person, editor's precision.
- **Do:** contractions, concrete nouns, numbers with sources, "publish-ready", "search intent".
- **Banned:** passionate, delve, leverage, synergy, unlock, elevate, "in today's fast-paced world", "I am writing to express".
- **Signature honesty:** ghostwritten work is labeled "ghostwritten for EaseUS" — credibility through transparency.

## Rules that hold the system
- Hairline rules are the structure; **no shadows, no gradients** anywhere.
- Equal left/right edges on every element.
- One accent moment per screen.
- Print conventions welcome: folio numbers, kickers, the end-mark `— 30 —`.
