# Research — writer/portfolio site patterns (Phase 0)

Sources: Colorlib "22 Best Content Writer Portfolio Examples", copyfol.io, twine.net, command-create.com (scraped summaries, 2026-11-18).

## What winning writer portfolios do
- **Name the client on every sample** — a link to the live article on the client's site beats a PDF (Elise Dopson, Lizzie Davey pattern).
- **Organize samples the way buyers search** — by industry/niche or content type, never by date (Jennifer Goforth Gregory by industry; Belinda Roozemond by type).
- **Prove results where possible** — rankings, CTR, traffic (Nathan Ojaokomo shows Google rank per article; Shruti has a real metric: "60% CTR lift").
- **Ghostwritten work handled openly** — label it, don't hide it (EaseUS ghostwriting → "ghostwritten for EaseUS" is credible).
- **Make hiring obvious** — services/packages, availability, one contact path.

## Common structure (proven flow)
1. Hero: what she writes + for whom + outcome + one CTA
2. Proof bar: brand names (Bragly.io, Truvora.ai, ImageWith.ai, TinyPNG, EaseUS, Phantom Rent A Car, TechnoSpot…) + 60% CTR stat + "20+ brands / 6+ yrs"
3. Featured work: 6-8 samples grouped by niche (SaaS/AI · Crypto/Web3 · E-commerce · Auto/Lifestyle)
4. Services: SEO blogs, landing pages, product/tool content, editing/content strategy
5. About: photo + bio + tech-fluent angle (MERN project, AI tools)
6. Contact: email + LinkedIn; optional form

## Wedge (what most writer portfolios do badly)
- Generic "I write words" positioning with no niche → Shruti's is sharp: **SEO content for SaaS/AI/tech + she can read the tech** (MERN, REST APIs — rare among writers).
- Samples as bare links with no context → each sample gets client + type + niche + result where known.
- No face / no trust signals → real photos exist (3 cutout PNGs already produced).
- Kitchen-sink samples → curate by target client (SaaS/AI/e-com first, crypto second).

## SEO keywords (target buyers' searches)
"SEO content writer", "freelance content writer India", "SaaS content writer", "AI content writer", "technical content writer", "B2B content writer", "hire content writer", "crypto content writer".

## Design directions shortlist (design-md)
- **A — Quiet editorial magazine** (elevenlabs-like): print-magazine calm, serif display — the site itself reads as a writing sample. Recommended.
- **B — Warm humanist** (claude-like): cream canvas, coral accent, serif display — warm, person-first.
- **C — Clean SaaS** (stripe/supabase-like): white + single accent — signals "I speak SaaS" to her biggest client niche.
